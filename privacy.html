NeverFap

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

The short version

  • We store your email, your streak data and your diary so the app can work. It's stored in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Other users can't see any of it. It is not end-to-end encrypted, so the people who run the database can technically reach it. Please read who can access your data.
  • We show no ads, don't sell your data and don't use tracking cookies. Anonymous page counts only run if you allow them.
  • You can delete diary entries at any time, and your whole account on request.

1. Who we are

NeverFap (neverfap.tommfr38.com) is run by Tomm, based in Hungary, who is the data controller for this service. Contact for anything in this policy: neverfap@tommfr38.com.

2. What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a secure hash. We can't read it.
  • Account timestamps, such as when you signed up and last signed in.

When you use the app

  • Streak data: when your current streak started, how many resets you've logged, your best flame stage and your achievements. The app saves this automatically every few seconds while you're logged in.
  • Diary entries: the text you write and when you wrote it.
  • A 6-digit code linked to your account, used to confirm deletion requests.

When you contact us

  • Your email address and whatever you write, for example in a bug report or a password help request.

Automatically, on every visit

  • Your IP address, browser details and the page requested. This reaches the providers in section 5 as a normal part of loading a website, and appears in their short-lived logs.

Only if you allow it

  • An anonymous page count: the page path, your language setting, the domain of the site that linked you here, and the date. It contains no IP address, no user ID and no exact time, so we can't connect it to you.

We don't ask for your name, location, contacts or payment details. Nothing you type goes to an AI service.

3. Your streak and diary are sensitive

Because NeverFap is about quitting porn, your streak data and diary can reveal information about your sex life and health. EU law treats this as a special category of data (GDPR Article 9), so we only process it with your explicit consent, which you give at sign-up.

You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account (section 8). We can't run the service without this data, so withdrawing means the account ends. Withdrawing doesn't affect what was lawfully done before.

5. Who can access your data

Other users: never. Database rules limit each account to its own streak and diary.

Us: your diary is stored as readable text in the database, encrypted while travelling to it and while stored on disk by our host, but not end-to-end encrypted. Anyone with full database access could technically read it. That means the operator and, in principle, the hosting provider. Our admin tools show your email, streak statistics and how many diary entries you have, but not what they say. We don't read your diary unless the law requires it or you ask us to because of a problem with your account.

6. International transfers

Your account, streak and diary data is stored in the EU. Some providers above are based in the United States, or may handle request data there. Where that happens, we rely on the provider's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, streak and diary: until you delete them or your account. We don't currently delete inactive accounts automatically.
  • Deleted data: a diary entry you delete is removed straight away. Account deletion is completed within 30 days of a verified request. Copies in provider backups can last up to about 30 more days.
  • Emails to us: until the matter is resolved, then deleted within 12 months.
  • Provider logs: short-term, as set by each provider.
  • Anonymous page counts: kept as statistics, since they can't identify anyone.

8. Your rights and how to delete your account

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time. Email neverfap@tommfr38.com from your account's address and we'll answer within one month.

To delete your account: go to Delete my account, confirm your login, and email us the 6-digit code it shows. This removes your account, streak data and diary.

To complain: you can contact the data protection authority where you live. In Hungary that's the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), naih.hu.

10. Security

We use HTTPS for all traffic, hashed passwords, encrypted storage at our database host and per-account database access rules. No online service is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we'll notify the authority and affected users as the law requires.

11. Age limit

NeverFap is only for people aged 18 or over. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we'll delete it. If you think that has happened, please email us.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
